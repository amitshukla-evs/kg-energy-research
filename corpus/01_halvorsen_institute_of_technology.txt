The Halvorsen Institute of Technology (HIT) is a public research university in Trondheim, Norway, founded in 1962. Its Department of Energy Systems hosts the Electrochemical Storage Laboratory, directed by Dr. Ingrid Solberg. HIT coordinates the ALPINE project, funded by the European Clean Energy Council, and is a partner in the CASCADE project led by Maple Ridge University. The institute employs about forty researchers in energy storage and has a long-standing industrial partnership with Fjordgrid AS, with which it jointly operates the Vestfjord Pilot Site. In June 2023 HIT hosted the Nordic Storage Symposium in Trondheim.
