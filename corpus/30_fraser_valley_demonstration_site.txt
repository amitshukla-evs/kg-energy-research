The Fraser Valley Demonstration Site is a field installation in British Columbia, Canada, commissioned in 2024. It combines a solar array with an 800 kilowatt-hour lithium iron phosphate battery. Maple Ridge University and Pacific Cell Technologies operate it together. Pacific Cell's dispatch software, based on forecasting algorithms from Dr. Daniel Okafor's group, controls the battery. The site was set up under the CASCADE project, funded by the Canadian Innovation Fund, and provided the data for the 2023 paper on probabilistic load forecasting and for later field trials of forecast-aware dispatch.
