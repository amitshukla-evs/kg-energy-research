RECLAIM develops economical processes for recovering valuable materials from end-of-life batteries, with emphasis on sodium-ion and lithium-ion cathodes. The project is funded by the European Clean Energy Council with 3.1 million euros and runs from 2022 to 2027. Rhine Valley University leads the work under principal investigator Dr. Markus Weber, and Lumen Storage Systems participates as industrial partner, supplying production scrap and used cells. Researchers use hydrometallurgical methods to extract metals and reuse them in new cells. One journal paper has been published so far.
