The Southern Peninsula Institute of Science (SPIS) is a public research institute in Chennai, India, established in 1985. Its Centre for Energy Materials is led by Dr. Priya Raghunathan, who is principal investigator of the MONSOON project funded by the National Science Endowment of India. SPIS operates the Tiruvallur Test Bed together with Deccan Power Corporation. Doctoral student Arjun Menon works at the Centre under Dr. Raghunathan. SPIS has an exchange agreement with Maple Ridge University and hosted the Asian Battery Safety Workshop in 2024. The institute focuses on batteries that remain safe and efficient in high ambient temperatures.
