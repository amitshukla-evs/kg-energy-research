Load forecasting is the prediction of electricity demand and renewable generation over a future time horizon. Probabilistic methods provide not only a single value but also a prediction interval that expresses uncertainty. Forecasts are used by utilities and storage operators to decide when to charge or discharge batteries. In this research network, load forecasting is the focus of the CASCADE project led by Maple Ridge University and is implemented in software by Pacific Cell Technologies. It is applied at the Fraser Valley Demonstration Site and is combined with degradation models in forecast-aware dispatch.
