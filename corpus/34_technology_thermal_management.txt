Thermal management of batteries comprises the methods used to keep cells within a safe temperature range during operation. Approaches include passive cooling with heat spreaders, active cooling with air or liquid circuits, and control strategies in the battery management system that reduce power when temperatures rise. Poor thermal management accelerates ageing and can create safety risks. In this research network, thermal management is the central topic of the MONSOON project and is evaluated at the Tiruvallur Test Bed. It is closely related to battery degradation modelling and to battery management software.
