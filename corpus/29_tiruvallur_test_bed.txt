The Tiruvallur Test Bed is an experimental storage facility near Chennai, India, commissioned in 2025. It contains a 1.5 megawatt-hour battery system with sodium-ion cells supplied by Lumen Storage Systems, housed in enclosures with different cooling configurations. The Southern Peninsula Institute of Science and Deccan Power Corporation operate the site jointly, with Rahul Venkatesan coordinating grid operations. It was built within the MONSOON project and is used to measure battery ageing at ambient temperatures above forty degrees Celsius. Measurements from the site underpin the 2024 paper on thermal management strategies.
