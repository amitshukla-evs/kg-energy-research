Pacific Cell Technologies is a Vancouver-based company founded in 2017 that develops battery management and dispatch software. Dr. Hannah Brooks, who completed her doctorate at Maple Ridge University under Dr. Daniel Okafor in 2018, heads the software division. Pacific Cell is an industrial partner in the CASCADE project and licenses a forecasting algorithm developed at Maple Ridge. Its software controls the storage units at the Fraser Valley Demonstration Site. The company also collaborates with the Halvorsen Institute of Technology on integrating forecast-aware control into sodium-ion battery systems.
