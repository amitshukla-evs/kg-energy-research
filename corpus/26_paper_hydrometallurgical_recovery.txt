Title: Hydrometallurgical Recovery of Sodium-Ion Cathode Materials. Published in 2024 in the Journal of Sustainable Materials Processing. Authors: Markus Weber and Katarina Novak. The paper describes a leaching and precipitation process that recovers more than ninety percent of transition metals from spent sodium-ion cathodes. It is the first publication of the RECLAIM project, funded by the European Clean Energy Council. The authors cite the 2022 paper on cathode stability to justify the choice of cathode chemistry. The study addresses battery recycling and was supported by used cells from Lumen Storage Systems.
