CASCADE applies machine learning to forecast renewable generation and to schedule battery charging and discharging. Funded by the Canadian Innovation Fund with 2.8 million Canadian dollars, it ran from 2022 to 2025. Maple Ridge University led the project under principal investigator Dr. Daniel Okafor. Partners were Pacific Cell Technologies, which integrated the algorithms into commercial software, and the Halvorsen Institute of Technology, which provided battery degradation models. Field trials were carried out at the Fraser Valley Demonstration Site, and the project generated two journal publications.
