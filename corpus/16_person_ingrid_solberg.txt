Dr. Ingrid Solberg is a professor at the Halvorsen Institute of Technology and director of its Electrochemical Storage Laboratory. She earned her doctorate at Rhine Valley University in 2005 under Dr. Markus Weber, joined HIT in 2008 and became full professor in 2016. She is principal investigator of the ALPINE project. Solberg supervised Dr. Tomas Berg, who graduated in 2016, and currently supervises doctoral student Sofia Lindqvist. She has co-authored papers on sodium-ion degradation and cathode stability, and gave a keynote at the Nordic Storage Symposium in 2023.
