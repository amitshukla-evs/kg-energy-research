Lumen Storage Systems is a battery manufacturer headquartered in Munich, Germany, founded in 2014, with around 320 employees. The company designs and builds stationary battery systems for grid and industrial customers. Dr. Elena Voss, previously a postdoctoral researcher at Rhine Valley University, has served as Chief Technology Officer since 2022. Lumen is an industrial partner in the ALPINE and RECLAIM projects and holds patent LSS-PAT-2020-114 on a sodium-ion cell design. The company supplied the battery cells installed at the Vestfjord Pilot Site and at the Tiruvallur Test Bed.
