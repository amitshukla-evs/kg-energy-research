Title: Forecast-Aware Dispatch of Grid Batteries. Published in 2025 in Energy Systems Control Letters. Authors: Hannah Brooks, Sofia Lindqvist and Daniel Okafor. The paper combines probabilistic load forecasts with a battery degradation model to schedule charging and discharging, reducing ageing by about twelve percent in simulation. It was produced within the CASCADE project. It cites the 2023 paper on probabilistic load forecasting for the forecasting method and the 2023 sodium-ion degradation study for the degradation model. The work links researchers at Pacific Cell Technologies, Maple Ridge University and the Halvorsen Institute of Technology.
