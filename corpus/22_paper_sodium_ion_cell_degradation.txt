Title: Sodium-Ion Cell Degradation Under Variable Grid Loads. Published in 2023 in the Journal of Energy Storage Engineering. Authors: Ingrid Solberg, Sofia Lindqvist and Tomas Berg. The paper reports operating data from the Vestfjord Pilot Site and shows how irregular charging patterns accelerate capacity loss in sodium-ion cells. It was produced within the ALPINE project and was presented at the Nordic Storage Symposium 2023. It builds on the 2022 paper on cathode stability in solid-state electrolytes, which it cites for the baseline material properties of the cells.
