The Vestfjord Pilot Site is a grid storage installation near Bergen, Norway, commissioned in 2024. It holds a two megawatt-hour sodium-ion battery system built from cells supplied by Lumen Storage Systems, whose design is covered by patent LSS-PAT-2020-114. The site is operated jointly by Fjordgrid AS and the Halvorsen Institute of Technology, with Dr. Tomas Berg as site manager. It was established under the ALPINE project and supports a local distribution network that suffers from congestion. Operating data from the site were used in the 2023 degradation study.
