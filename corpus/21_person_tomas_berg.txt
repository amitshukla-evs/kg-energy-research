Dr. Tomas Berg is lead storage engineer at Fjordgrid AS in Bergen. He completed his doctorate at the Halvorsen Institute of Technology in 2016 under Dr. Ingrid Solberg and moved directly to Fjordgrid. He manages operations at the Vestfjord Pilot Site and acts as the utility's representative in the ALPINE project. Berg contributes real-world operating data to academic studies and co-authored the 2023 paper on sodium-ion cell degradation with Solberg and Sofia Lindqvist. His work focuses on grid congestion and the economics of local storage in remote communities.
