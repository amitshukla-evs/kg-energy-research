Title: Sodium-Ion Cathode Stability in Solid-State Electrolytes. Published in 2022 in Advanced Electrochemical Materials. Authors: Katarina Novak, Ingrid Solberg and Elena Voss. The study examines how a composite electrolyte interface limits cathode degradation during repeated cycling. Its findings underpin the cell design covered by patent LSS-PAT-2020-114, held by Lumen Storage Systems. The work was carried out within the ALPINE project, funded by the European Clean Energy Council, and the topics addressed are sodium-ion batteries and solid-state electrolytes. The paper has been cited by later studies on degradation and recycling.
