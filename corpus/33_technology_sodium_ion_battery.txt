A sodium-ion battery is a type of rechargeable electrochemical storage device that moves sodium ions between a cathode and an anode through an electrolyte. It belongs to the broader category of rechargeable batteries, alongside lithium-ion batteries and lithium iron phosphate batteries. Sodium is abundant and inexpensive, which makes the technology attractive for stationary grid storage. Typical research questions concern cathode stability, electrolyte design, cycle life and recycling. In this research network, sodium-ion batteries are studied in the ALPINE and MONSOON projects and recycled in RECLAIM.
