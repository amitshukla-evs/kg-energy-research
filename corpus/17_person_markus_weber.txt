Dr. Markus Weber is a professor of materials engineering at Rhine Valley University and head of its Institute for Materials and Recycling. He is principal investigator of the RECLAIM project and a senior contributor to ALPINE through his group. Weber supervised Dr. Ingrid Solberg during her doctorate, completed in 2005, and later hosted Dr. Elena Voss as a postdoctoral researcher from 2015 to 2019. His research addresses metal recovery and sustainable battery materials. He co-authored a 2024 paper on hydrometallurgical recovery with Dr. Katarina Novak.
