The European Clean Energy Council (ECEC) is a funding agency based in Brussels, established in 2010 to support applied research on decarbonisation and grid flexibility. It funds the ALPINE project, led by the Halvorsen Institute of Technology, with a grant of 4.2 million euros for 2021 to 2025. It also funds the RECLAIM project, led by Rhine Valley University, with 3.1 million euros for 2022 to 2027. ECEC requires funded consortia to include at least one industrial partner and to publish results in open-access journals.
