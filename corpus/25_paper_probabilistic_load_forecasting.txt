Title: Probabilistic Load Forecasting with Gradient-Boosted Models. Published in 2023 in the IEEE-style journal Transactions on Smart Grid Analytics. Authors: Daniel Okafor, Li Wei and Hannah Brooks. The paper introduces a forecasting method that outputs prediction intervals for regional electricity demand and solar generation. It was produced within the CASCADE project and evaluated on data from the Fraser Valley Demonstration Site. The algorithm was later licensed to Pacific Cell Technologies. Okafor presented the results at the Nordic Storage Symposium 2023. The central topic is load forecasting.
