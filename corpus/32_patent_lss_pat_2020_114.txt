Patent LSS-PAT-2020-114, titled Layered Sodium-Ion Cell with Composite Electrolyte Interface, was filed in 2020 and granted in 2022. The patent holder is Lumen Storage Systems in Munich, Germany. The named inventors are Dr. Elena Voss and Dr. Katarina Novak of Rhine Valley University. The invention describes a sodium-ion cell in which a composite interface stabilises the cathode. Scientific background is reported in the 2022 paper on cathode stability. Cells built to this design are used at the Vestfjord Pilot Site and the Tiruvallur Test Bed, and the patent is licensed within the ALPINE project.
