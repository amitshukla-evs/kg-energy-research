Title: Thermal Management Strategies for Stationary Batteries in Hot Climates. Published in 2024 in the International Journal of Thermal Energy Systems. Authors: Priya Raghunathan, Arjun Menon and Daniel Okafor. Using measurements from the Tiruvallur Test Bed, the paper compares passive and active cooling for sodium-ion battery enclosures at ambient temperatures above forty degrees Celsius. It is the first publication of the MONSOON project and cites the 2023 study of sodium-ion cell degradation for its ageing model. The research topic is thermal management of stationary storage.
