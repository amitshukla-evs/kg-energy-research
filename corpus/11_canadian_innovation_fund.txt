The Canadian Innovation Fund (CIF) is a federal funding programme headquartered in Ottawa that finances partnerships between universities and industry. It funds the CASCADE project, led by Maple Ridge University, with a grant of 2.8 million Canadian dollars for 2022 to 2025. CASCADE brings together academic researchers and the company Pacific Cell Technologies, with the Halvorsen Institute of Technology participating as an international research partner. CIF evaluates projects on commercial readiness and requires a demonstration phase, which in CASCADE takes place at the Fraser Valley Demonstration Site.
