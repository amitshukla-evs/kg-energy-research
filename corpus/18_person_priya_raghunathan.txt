Dr. Priya Raghunathan is an associate professor at the Southern Peninsula Institute of Science and leads its Centre for Energy Materials. She is principal investigator of the MONSOON project. In 2019 she spent a sabbatical as visiting scholar at Maple Ridge University, working with Dr. Daniel Okafor, which led to the later partnership between the two institutions. She supervises doctoral student Arjun Menon. Her research concerns thermal behaviour and safety of stationary batteries, and she co-authored a 2024 publication on thermal management with Menon and Okafor.
