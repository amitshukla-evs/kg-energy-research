Dr. Daniel Okafor is a professor at Maple Ridge University and leads the Smart Grid Analytics Group. He is principal investigator of the CASCADE project and a collaborator in MONSOON. In 2018 he supervised the doctoral thesis of Dr. Hannah Brooks, now at Pacific Cell Technologies. He worked with Dr. Priya Raghunathan during her 2019 visit and works closely with research scientist Dr. Li Wei. His research covers probabilistic load forecasting and battery dispatch. He presented CASCADE results at the Nordic Storage Symposium in 2023.
