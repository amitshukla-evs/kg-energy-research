Deccan Power Corporation is a state-owned electricity utility headquartered in Hyderabad, India, supplying power to several southern districts. Project manager Rahul Venkatesan represents the company in the MONSOON project, led by the Southern Peninsula Institute of Science. Deccan Power co-operates the Tiruvallur Test Bed with SPIS and provides grid connection and operational data. The utility is motivated by summer peak demand and frequent temperatures above forty degrees Celsius, which accelerate battery ageing. It plans to deploy stationary storage at several substations if the MONSOON results prove favourable.
