The Nordic Storage Symposium 2023 was held in Trondheim, Norway, from 14 to 16 June 2023 and was hosted by the Halvorsen Institute of Technology. About two hundred participants from academia and industry attended. Dr. Ingrid Solberg gave the keynote presentation on sodium-ion degradation, based on the paper written with Sofia Lindqvist and Tomas Berg. Dr. Elena Voss took part in an industry panel on manufacturing, and Dr. Daniel Okafor presented the forecasting results of the CASCADE project. Dr. Markus Weber attended as an invited guest. The symposium led to the first discussions on a joint forecasting study.
