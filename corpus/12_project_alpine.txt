ALPINE, a project on sodium-ion storage for Alpine and Nordic grids, ran from 2021 to 2025 with funding of 4.2 million euros from the European Clean Energy Council. The Halvorsen Institute of Technology coordinated the consortium, with Dr. Ingrid Solberg as principal investigator. Partners were Rhine Valley University, which developed solid-state electrolytes, Lumen Storage Systems, which manufactured the cells, and Fjordgrid AS, which operated the pilot. The work produced the sodium-ion battery installed at the Vestfjord Pilot Site and two journal publications on cell stability and degradation.
