Maple Ridge University is a public university in Vancouver, Canada, founded in 1968. Its Faculty of Applied Science includes the Smart Grid Analytics Group, led by Dr. Daniel Okafor, where research scientist Dr. Li Wei develops forecasting models. Maple Ridge leads the CASCADE project, funded by the Canadian Innovation Fund, and is a partner in the MONSOON project led by the Southern Peninsula Institute of Science. The university operates the Fraser Valley Demonstration Site with Pacific Cell Technologies. Dr. Hannah Brooks completed her doctorate at Maple Ridge in 2018 and then joined Pacific Cell Technologies.
