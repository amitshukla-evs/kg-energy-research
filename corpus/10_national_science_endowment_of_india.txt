The National Science Endowment of India (NSEI) is a government funding body based in New Delhi that supports research in energy, materials and public health. It funds the MONSOON project, led by the Southern Peninsula Institute of Science, with a grant of 380 million rupees for the period 2022 to 2027. NSEI also awards doctoral fellowships; Arjun Menon of SPIS holds one such fellowship. The endowment encourages international collaboration and has approved the participation of Maple Ridge University as a foreign partner in MONSOON.
