Dr. Elena Voss is Chief Technology Officer of Lumen Storage Systems in Munich. She was a postdoctoral researcher at Rhine Valley University from 2015 to 2019 under Dr. Markus Weber and then joined Lumen as a senior engineer, becoming CTO in 2022. Together with Dr. Katarina Novak she is a named inventor on patent LSS-PAT-2020-114. She represents Lumen in the ALPINE and RECLAIM projects and co-authored a 2022 paper on cathode stability in solid-state electrolytes. She spoke on an industry panel at the Nordic Storage Symposium.
