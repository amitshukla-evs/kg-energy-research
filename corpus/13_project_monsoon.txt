MONSOON investigates stationary batteries that remain reliable in hot and humid climates. The project runs from 2022 to 2027 and is funded by the National Science Endowment of India. The Southern Peninsula Institute of Science leads the project, with Dr. Priya Raghunathan as principal investigator. Partners are Deccan Power Corporation, represented by Rahul Venkatesan, and Maple Ridge University, which contributes modelling expertise. Experiments take place at the Tiruvallur Test Bed. Doctoral student Arjun Menon studies thermal management strategies, and the project's first publication appeared in 2024.
