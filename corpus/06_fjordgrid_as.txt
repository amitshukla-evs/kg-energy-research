Fjordgrid AS is a regional electricity distribution utility based in Bergen, Norway, serving communities along the western coast. The company is a partner in the ALPINE project and operates the Vestfjord Pilot Site jointly with the Halvorsen Institute of Technology. Dr. Tomas Berg, Fjordgrid's lead storage engineer, earned his doctorate at HIT in 2016 under Dr. Ingrid Solberg and joined the utility the same year. Fjordgrid is interested in storage that reduces grid congestion in remote fjord communities, and it installs and monitors battery systems supplied by Lumen Storage Systems.
