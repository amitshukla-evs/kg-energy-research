Rhine Valley University (RVU) is a public university in Karlsruhe, Germany, founded in 1957. Its Institute for Materials and Recycling is headed by Dr. Markus Weber, who also leads the RECLAIM project on battery recycling, funded by the European Clean Energy Council. RVU is a partner in the ALPINE project, where senior researcher Dr. Katarina Novak develops solid-state electrolytes. Dr. Elena Voss worked at RVU as a postdoctoral researcher until 2019 before joining Lumen Storage Systems. RVU maintains a collaboration agreement with Lumen and regularly places doctoral graduates in German and Nordic industry and academia.
